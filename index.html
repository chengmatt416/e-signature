How It Works

1. Create a Signature: Visit the signing page, draw your signature, and the system automatically captures the date, time, and device information.

2. Generate Sign-ID: A unique Sign-ID is generated for each signature. This Sign-ID acts as your decryption key - keep it safe!

3. Encryption: All data is encrypted using a secure master key combined with your Sign-ID before being exported as a .esig file. The Sign-ID is NOT stored in the file.

4. Decrypt & Verify: Upload the .esig file and enter your Sign-ID to decrypt and verify the signature's authenticity. Without the correct Sign-ID, the file cannot be decrypted.

🔐 Privacy First: All processing happens entirely in your browser. No data is sent to any server. Your signatures and data remain completely private. Only you know your Sign-ID.
